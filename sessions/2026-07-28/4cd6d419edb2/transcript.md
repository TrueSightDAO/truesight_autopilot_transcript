## Boosting Epicatechin & Flavanols in Cacao — Variety, Cultivation & Processing

### 1. Genetics — Variety Matters More Than Anything

| Variety | Epicatechin/Flavanol Content | Key Trait |
|---|---|---|
| **Criollo** | **Highest** — up to 3x more flavonoids than Forastero | Rare (3-5% of world crop), delicate, prized for flavor |
| **Nacional** (Arriba) | **Very high** — unique florals, high polyphenols | Ecuador, ancestral variety |
| **Trinitario** | **High** (hybrid vigor) — 1.5-2x Forastero | Cross of Criollo + Forastero, disease-resistant |
| **Forastero** (Amazonian/Amelonado) | **Lowest** — bulk cocoa, low flavanols | 80%+ of world production |

**Key study (PMC 2021):** *"Significant differences between varieties: the highest content of flavanol (+)-catechin and (−)-epicatechin was observed in Criollo, followed by Trinitario. Forastero samples contained less than half the epicatechin of Criollo."*

### 2. Geography — Terroir Effects

| Factor | Effect on Epicatechin | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Altitude (600-900m)** | **+30-50%** vs lowland (<300m) | PMC study: altitude was the strongest single predictor |
