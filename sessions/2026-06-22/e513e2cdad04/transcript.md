## Deep Dive: Key Differences

### 1. Theory vs. Practice

**ITKN** is a **white paper project** — a beautifully designed Squarespace site with a compelling narrative ("The Holy Grail of Impact Investing"), impressive advisor names (Jed Emerson), and a grand vision. But there is **zero evidence of execution**: no token contract, no GitHub, no product, no revenue, no community. The site hasn't been meaningfully updated since ~2018. It's a pitch deck, not a protocol.

**TrueSight DAO** is the opposite — rough edges, smaller names, but **shipping**. Real cacao moving through a real supply chain. QR codes on real bags. Real Stripe transactions. A working DAO API (Edgar). A ledger that updates daily. The whitepaper is a living document on truesight.me, not a PDF behind an email gate.

### 2. Token Model: Buy vs. Earn

| | ITKN | TDG |
|---|---|---|
| **How you get it** | Buy in a token sale (presumably) | **Earn it** — 1 TDG per $1 contributed, 100 TDG per hour |
| **Value backing** | Speculative / narrative-driven | **Profit-backed** — real business profits buy back TDG, creating a floor |
| **Incentive alignment** | Investor → hopes price goes up | Contributor → builds the DAO → DAO profits → TDG value rises |
| **Governance** | Not mentioned | Core feature — TDG = voting rights on fund allocation |

This is the **single most important difference**. ITKN is a traditional ICO-era token: buy it, hope it appreciates. TDG is a **contribution-backed governance token**: you *earn* it by doing real work, and the value comes from the DAO's real profits buying it back.

### 3. Real-World Asset Backing

**ITKN** has no visible connection to any real-world asset, business, or revenue stream. The entire value proposition rests on "monetizing social impact" — a concept that remains undefined in the public materials.

**TrueSight DAO** has:
- **Physical inventory**: Ceremonial cacao in warehouses, tracked by QR code
- **Revenue**: Stripe sales to end customers
- **Supply chain**: Farmer purchase agreements, freight logistics, consignment
- **Asset-per-TDG ratio**: Calculated daily from the off-chain asset balance
- **Buy-back mechanism**: Daily budget calculated from treasury yield

1. **Narrative polish.** The "Mining Good" concept is elegant. "Capturing and monetizing the creation of social good" is a powerful framing. Our "heal the world with love" is beautiful but less immediately graspable as an investment thesis.

1. **Execution > narrative.** ITKN had the story but no product. We have the product but need a better story.

2. **Earned tokens > bought tokens.** The contribution-based model creates real alignment. ICO tokens attract speculators; earned tokens attract builders.

3. **Real revenue > impact theater.** Cacao is a real business with real margins. "Impact" without revenue is charity; impact with revenue is sustainable.

4. **Transparency by default.** Our Google Sheets ledger is more transparent than 99% of crypto projects. That's a moat.

5. **QR-coded physical supply chain.** This is genuinely novel — bridging physical cacao bags to on-chain (or on-sheet) provenance. ITKN had nothing like this.
