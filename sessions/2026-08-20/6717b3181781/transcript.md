## The short answer
Yes, several groups are building **barges, boats, and floating structures whose material IS recycled plastic waste** — including ocean/canal/river plastic. The closest match to what you're describing is **Eco Brixs in Uganda**, which built a **pilot cargo barge from waste plastic** that has already done real collection work. Nobody is yet doing this at industrial scale for seagoing cargo, but the demonstration tier is well-proven.

## The standout: Eco Brixs (Uganda) — a real cargo barge from plastic waste
- Built a **pilot cargo barge from 100% recycled plastic waste** (modular hull), float-tested it, then ran it to **Faza Island** where it returned with **850+ kg of collected waste plastics** on its first trip — a true closed loop (barge made of plastic, hauling plastic).
- Also built Masaka's **first boat from recycled plastic** (300 kg capacity; reportedly ~500-year lifespan vs wooden boats).
- They're a social enterprise: local collectors (many women) supply the waste; recycling creates income in marginalized communities.

## Who else is doing this (by category)

**A. Vessels built FROM recycled plastic waste** (material = the waste)

**B. Barges that COLLECT plastic** (conventional material, job is scooping) — important distinction, often confused
- **The Ocean Cleanup "Interceptor"** — solar barges capturing river plastic before it reaches oceans (21 deployed in 10 countries; ~$1.3M design + $1.5M per site)
- **4ocean** — platform barges hauling ~20,000 lbs of recovered trash per run in Guatemala
- Bangkok's solar canal-cleaning barge (Mongabay, 2026)

**C. Floating structures / parks / islands from recycled plastic**
- **Recycled Park, Rotterdam** — first floating park built from recycled river plastic (WHIM Architecture + Recycled Island Foundation); full habitable "Recycled Island" remains a concept
- **Joysxee (Mexico)** — Richart Sowa's floating island on ~125k–250k bottles, registered as an "ecological boat"
- **Abidjan Lagoon resort (Ivory Coast)** — island resort floating on 700,000 bottles
- **Ocean Vortex** — 2026 concept: floating parliament building from recycled marine waste (competition finalist, unbuilt)

**D. Supporting / adjacent tech**
- **Plastic Odyssey** — 40m lab vessel with an onboard recycling microfactory; tours the 30 most plastic-polluted coastal cities incubating recycling entrepreneurs (departed Marseille 2022)
- **ByFusion** — turned 20 tons of Ocean Voyages Institute's Pacific gyre catch into "ByBlock" construction blocks; buildable material that could theoretically form floating structures

## State of the field
- **Proven**: recycled-plastic boatbuilding at small/commercial scale (Flipflopi taxi dhows, Plastic Whale fleet, HDPE workboats/barges). Material is genuinely durable — HDPE hulls outlast wood and fiberglass, are fully recyclable, and don't corrode.
- **Not yet done**: large ocean-going cargo barges from actual ocean-gyre plastic at industrial scale. The economics are the blocker — ocean plastic is degraded (UV/salt), requires heavy sorting/cleaning, and virgin HDPE is cheap. That's why most ocean-plastic builds are statement/awareness vessels or community-scale loops like Eco Brixs.

**One-liner takeaway:** If you're thinking about this for the DAO's own logistics (e.g. river freight around cacao-growing regions), Eco Brixs and Flipflopi are the models to study — both prove the closed loop works at community scale.
