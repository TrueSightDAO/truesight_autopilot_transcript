Yes — this is possible, and there are two ways to think about it depending on what you mean by "login directly":

## What exists
- **Meta Business Portfolio (formerly "Business Manager")** — the "business account" layer at business.facebook.com / Meta Business Suite. It holds your Facebook Page, Instagram, ad accounts, etc. under one umbrella.
- **People log into it directly** — each person who is invited gets their own login to business.facebook.com and manages the business account/portfolio without sharing credentials.

## Two paths to "login directly"

**1. Standard path (what most small businesses do):**
- One person (e.g. you) creates the Business Portfolio. This step currently requires a **personal Facebook account** — there's no way to create a portfolio with zero Facebook profile.
- Then add people: *Business Suite → Settings → Users → Invite people* — invite by email (email must match the invitee's Facebook account), assign roles (Admin / Employee) and which assets they can touch.
- Result: everyone logs in directly with their **own** Facebook/email credentials and manages the same business account. Nobody shares passwords.

**2. Managed Meta Accounts (the "no personal Facebook needed" option):**
- Meta now has **work-only accounts** (formerly "Meta for Work") that let employees access Business Suite / Ads Manager using company credentials or SSO (Google Workspace, Okta, Azure AD) — **no personal Facebook profile required**.
- Caveat: this is **invitation-only, rolled out to selected organizations** — individuals can't self-enroll. You'd need to request eligibility via Meta support / your Meta representative.

## Practical recommendation for your use case
- If this is a small org (e.g. a few people managing one Page): **Path 1** is what you want. Create the portfolio, invite your team by email, assign roles. They log in directly with their own accounts and can manage it end-to-end.
- If you specifically need **no one to have/use a personal Facebook account at all**, that's Path 2 — and it requires Meta to enable managed accounts for your organization first.
