## Brazil → China GACC Registration Guide

**Prepared:** 16 June 2026 by Sophia Truesight
**Companion PDF:** `exports/2026-06-16_gacc_registration_black_king_china.pdf`

### What this covers

The complete regulatory pathway for importing Black King cacao from Ilhéus, BA (Brazil) to Dongguan, China.

### Key takeaways

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Regulation** | GACC Decree No. 280 (effective June 1, 2026) — replaces No. 248 |
| **Big change** | Unroasted cocoa beans **removed** from the "Official Recommendation" list → **Self-application (Pathway 2)** via CIFER. No MAPA recommendation letter needed. |
| **Supplier** | Black King (Matheus Reis Pereira) — CNPJ 50.042.585/0001-80 |
| **GACC status** | **NOT YET OBTAINED** — critical path item |
| **Timeline** | 3–6 months for GACC registration; first shipment ~6–8 months from start |
| **Fallback** | Coopercabruca (Itabuna, BA) — same region, established exporter |

### 10 clearance documents required

1. Certificate of Origin (CAMEX)
2. Health/Sanitary Certificate (MAPA)
3. Production/Packaging Date Certificate (Black King)
4. Ingredient List
5. Composition/Test Report (accredited lab)
6. **Bilingual labels (Chinese + English)** — must include GACC number
7. **GACC Registration Number** — must be obtained first
8. Phytosanitary Certificate (MAPA) — for unroasted beans/almonds
9. Commercial Invoice & Packing List
10. Bill of Lading / Air Waybill

### Two shipment categories

- **Category A (Event Samples):** ~27 units, ~8.8 kg net, ~$200–300 USD — promotional
- **Category B (Commercial):** ~611 units, ~351 kg net, ~$7,032.53 USD FOB — 11 product SKUs

### Recommendation

Start GACC registration **immediately** — it's the critical path at 3–6 months. The CIFER portal is at **cifer.singlewindow.cn**.
