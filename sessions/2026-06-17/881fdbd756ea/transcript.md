6. **Bilingual labels (Chinese + English)** — must include GACC number
7. **GACC Registration Number** — must be obtained first
8. Phytosanitary Certificate (MAPA) — for unroasted beans/almonds
9. Commercial Invoice & Packing List
10. Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
